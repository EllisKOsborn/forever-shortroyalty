Short Royalty · v0.6.0 redesign · step 1

A calmer Short Royalty

What's crowded today, and three ways to fix it. The mock-ups below are live: switch screens, colours and fonts to compare. Nothing in the addon changes until you pick.

1 · What's cluttered now

Pictures of today's screens (the preview of v0.5.7), with what competes for your eye and what could hide until you need it.

On every screen

  • Three bright colours compete: gold for "where you are", pink for "what you picked" and orange for alerts. Even the healthy sync dot is orange.
  • The title bar repeats the name ("SHORT ROYALTY … Short Royalty · Sync on").
  • Boxes everywhere: buttons, chips, menus and slots all wear the same outline, so nothing looks more clickable than anything else.

3 · My recommendation

B, the sidebar, with an accent choice recommended

  • Professions needs two levels (Guild, Recipes, Cooldowns, and Levelling when it comes back). A sidebar plus page tabs gives each level its own look, so they stop looking like filter chips.
  • New sections (Levelling, Raids if it returns) get a line in the sidebar instead of squeezing the tab row.
  • Each page gets a title and one sentence saying what it's for, which helps guildmates who open it once a week.
  • The cost: the sidebar takes about a fifth of the width, so Roster shows 6 columns instead of 9. Rank, version and data age move to the name's tooltip.

Themes later, and small optional

  • Start with the accent choice (pink crest, gold or your class colour) and the transparent window. They're cheap and cover what most people want.
  • Each extra theme has to be checked for readable text and alert colours on every screen, so I'd add Midnight and Forge only if guildmates ask.
  • EllesmereUI's accent: I'll only follow it if its code offers a public way to read it. That's checked during the build, not promised.
