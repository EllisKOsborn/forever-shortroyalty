Everything in the window uses the addon's own values: colours, sizes, spacing and motion timings are generated from UI/Theme.lua (node tools/preview-tokens.js), and the title font is the bundled Chakra Petch. Item icons are game files, so gear slots show a plain tile instead. Tooltips are the game's own (approximated here). All names and numbers are made up. "Before" pictures are the v0.5.7 look, from docs/images/before-v0.6/.
